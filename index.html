Gameplay

Think across the whole board.

There is no time limit. Plan your next move by predicting how the blocks will shift. The key is to look at the whole board and connect matching colors before you run out of space.

Core rules

Simple controls. Plenty to think about.

Every swipe changes the entire board. One move can open up new opportunities—or limit your options. Think ahead and plan your next move.

Swipe

Move every block up, down, left, or right at the same time.

Read what comes next

New blocks enter from the opposite side in random positions.

Connect four

Join four or more blocks of the same color to clear them and score.

Adapt

New colors appear as the score rises, increasing the puzzle’s complexity.

Beyond the score

Every cat leaves something behind.

As you clear blocks, you'll encounter a variety of cats in the world of CellCats.

Discover and befriend cats

Clear more of each cat's blocks to become friends. Once you've befriended a cat, it joins your collection, and you can choose it to play with in future games.

Uncover their memories

As your friendship with each cat grows, you'll unlock fragments of its memories. Collect these fragments to gradually uncover the stories each cat holds within.

Creative intent

A puzzle about connection.

CellCats explores the connections between cats and people, while also touching on the challenges cats face in the world today. The bright, playful puzzle world contrasts with the quiet world of the cats' memories. Through this contrast, I hope to create an experience that stays with players long after they finish playing.

I care deeply about the welfare and protection of cats. In the future, I hope to donate a portion of CellCats' revenue to organizations dedicated to cat rescue and welfare.

Developer

Koji Takamiya
